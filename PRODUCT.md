# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Postgres (Neon) + Drizzle, deployed on Vercel, installable as a PWA on the phone. Chosen with the user ("the most interesting option to save progress, be easy to reach from the phone, and give each person their own login"); same combo he already runs in another project.

## Users

Nathan and his friends, a mix of people who already train and beginners. Each person has their own account. The primary scene is the phone in one hand between sets at the gym: quick glances during the rest interval, one-handed taps, sweaty fingers. Secondary scene: at home, building or importing a workout plan and looking at progress.

## Product Purpose

A personal workout tracker inspired by Befit (appbefit.com), for private use among friends. It holds each person's workout plan, lets them log load and reps set by set, log cardio, follow progress over time, see how each exercise is performed, swap an exercise for an equivalent one, and, most importantly, bring in their own plan. Success: logging a whole session takes less attention than the old notes app or paper sheet, and progress is visible without effort.

## Positioning

Your own plan first. Unlike Befit, the plan is not generated by an AI coach; the user brings the plan they already follow (built in the app, pasted as text, or read from a photo/PDF of the trainer's sheet) and the app turns it into a live logging tool with demonstrations, swaps and history.

## Operating Context

- Gym plans in Brazil are written as split sheets: Treino A / B / C, exercise name, sets x reps (e.g. "Supino reto 4x10-12"), sometimes rest and load.
- Trainers hand out sheets on paper, in PDF, or as photos; people also keep them in notes apps.
- Rest between sets is typically 45 s to 3 min; that gap is when the app is used.
- Exercise demonstrations come from the free ExerciseDB V1 catalog (1,500 exercises, animated GIFs), with names translated to Brazilian gym vocabulary.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Accounts per person (email + password), optional invite code so only friends sign up.
- Plans with days (A/B/C…), exercises with sets, rep range, rest and notes; multiple plans, one active; share a plan with a friend by code; starter templates.
- Import: build in-app, paste free text (parsed), photo/PDF of the sheet (read by Claude API; requires an API key, has a per-use cost).
- Live session: next day in sequence, previous performance per set, set types (warm-up, normal, drop, failure), rest timer with sound/vibration, swap exercise for one with the same target muscle, add/remove exercises, notes, finish summary with PRs.
- Cardio log (type, duration, distance, calories, intensity) inside a session or standalone.
- Progress: history, per-exercise charts (best set, estimated 1RM, volume), personal records, weekly goal and streak, weekly volume per muscle, muscle recovery estimate, body weight log.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese only. Units: kg, km.
- Exercise media is hotlinked from ExerciseDB's CDN (© its owners); instructions are in English unless translated on demand by the AI key.

## Evidence on Hand

- No logo, imagery, testimonials or real user data exist. Do not fabricate testimonials, user counts or claims.

## Product Principles

1. The set is the unit: the fastest path in the app is "enter weight, enter reps, done".
2. Bring your own plan: importing and editing a plan must never feel like a chore.
3. Show, don't tell: every exercise has its demonstration one tap away, which matters for the beginners in the group.
4. Progress without bookkeeping: everything logged turns into history and charts automatically.
5. Friends, not a platform: no social feed, no gamified pressure; sharing is a code you send to a friend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large touch targets for one-handed use with sweaty fingers; numbers readable at arm's length; reduced-motion respected; contrast sufficient for bright gym lighting.

## Visual Commitment

The user chose the category standard ("padrão de app fitness, mas mais feminino"): the familiar fitness-app canon (dark-first, rounded cards, one vibrant accent, in the family of Befit and Hevy) played straight, re-tuned with a softer, feminine palette and warmth. Benchmark products assumed (not named by the user): Befit, Hevy, Sweat.
